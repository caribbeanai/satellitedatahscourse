## Before you start

Work through all four student notebooks in the [`../notebooks/`](../notebooks/)
folder first. The exam draws on every skill from Missions 1 to 4:

- Building a bounding box and searching the Sentinel-2 archive
- Checking coverage over your own study area, not just the whole tile
- Computing NDVI, MNDWI, NDBI, and NBR from Sentinel-2 bands
- Building median composites to remove cloud
- Comparing before and after periods around a dated event
- Running a control period to separate signal from seasonal noise
- Pulling NASA POWER climate records and fitting a warming trend
- Reporting a trend against its own uncertainty, honestly
